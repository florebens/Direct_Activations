It is one of the hardest transits of evolution, and this is why so many eyes are upon us.

The Advanced Civilizations assisting us, want to ask what is the Vision that we hold for Humanity and all its potential. Where are we stewarding this amazing ship? How are we planning to do so? 

The Ontological Project of Humanity is in our hands, and this particular moment in time is extremely sensitive. The CACL are inviting us to the table. Are we up for the challenge?
